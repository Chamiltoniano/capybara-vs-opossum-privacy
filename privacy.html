2. Accounts and information provided directly by users

Capybara vs Opossum does not require users to create a Chamiltongames account or sign in to play.

Chamiltongames does not require players to provide their name, home address, telephone number, personal photographs, payment information or other similar personal information in order to play the game.

Capybara vs Opossum is free to play and is supported by advertising. The game does not contain in-app purchases.

The game uses Google AdMob, a service provided by Google, to display advertisements.

As a consequence of using Google advertising services, Google and advertising technology providers used through those services may collect, receive, process or share information according to their respective privacy policies and the privacy choices available to the user.

  • Interactions with the application.
  • Interactions with advertisements.
  • Diagnostic information.
  • App performance and technical information.
  • Device, operating system and application information.

Capybara vs Opossum does not request access to the user's precise GPS location for advertising purposes.
