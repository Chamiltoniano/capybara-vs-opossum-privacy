Data & Consent

A plain-language overview of data and advertising in Capybara vs Opossum.

Do I need an account?

No. Capybara vs Opossum does not require users to create an account or sign in to play.

Does the game ask for my name, address or phone number?

No. This information is not required to play the game.

Does the game contain advertising?

Yes. Capybara vs Opossum is free to play and is supported by advertising. The game does not contain in-app purchases.

What advertising service does the game use?

The game uses Google AdMob to provide advertisements. AdMob and related Google services may process information such as device identifiers, approximate location, interactions with the app, diagnostic information and other technical information.

Why may this information be processed?

Depending on the user's region, device settings and privacy choices, information may be processed for purposes such as providing advertising, analytics, measuring app performance, preventing fraud and complying with legal obligations.

Will I be asked for consent?

Where required by applicable law or platform requirements, users may be presented with privacy or consent choices related to advertising and data processing.

Can I change my privacy choices?

Available privacy choices may depend on the user's region, device settings and the privacy controls provided by Google and the Android platform.

Does Chamiltongames directly collect personal information?

Chamiltongames does not require users to provide personal information such as their name, postal address or phone number in order to play the game. Third-party services integrated into the game may process technical, advertising, usage or diagnostic information as described above.

Where can I find more information?

Please read our Privacy Policy for more detailed information about privacy and third-party services used by the game.
